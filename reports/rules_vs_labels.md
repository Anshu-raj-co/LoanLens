# Policy rules vs historical approval labels

Generated by `python -m loanlens.rules` from `data/processed/loan_approval_clean.csv` (4,269 applicants: 2,656 approved, 1,613 rejected).

All thresholds are **placeholders** from `config/policy.yaml`, illustrative until replaced by a lender's real credit policy. The labels are *approval decisions* in synthetic data, not repayment outcomes, so this table shows agreement with past decisions, not risk.

EMI assumes 10.0% annual interest (placeholder; the dataset has no rate).

## Share of applicants each rule would reject

| Rule | Threshold | Fails (all) | Fails among approved | Fails among rejected | Approval rate when failing | Implied enforce (<5% of approved) | Configured enforce |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `min_cibil` | 550 | 1,785 (41.8%) | 185 (7.0%) | 1,600 (99.2%) | 10.4% | True | True |
| `max_emi_to_income` | 0.5 | 1,877 (44.0%) | 1,260 (47.4%) | 617 (38.3%) | 67.1% | False | False |
| `max_loan_to_income` | 4 | 0 (0.0%) | 0 (0.0%) | 0 (0.0%) | n/a | True | True |
| `max_loan_to_assets` | 1.5 | 0 (0.0%) | 0 (0.0%) | 0 (0.0%) | n/a | True | True |
| `min_loan_term_years` | 2 | 0 (0.0%) | 0 (0.0%) | 0 (0.0%) | n/a | True | True |
| `max_loan_term_years` | 20 | 0 (0.0%) | 0 (0.0%) | 0 (0.0%) | n/a | True | True |

"Implied enforce" applies the review rule to the three ratio rules: enforce only if the rule would reject fewer than 5% of applicants who were actually approved. `min_cibil` and the loan-term limits are enforced by decision regardless.

## Enforcement choices

- `min_cibil`: enforce = **True** (enforced by review decision; it would reject 185 applicants (7.0%) who were approved in the data).
- `max_emi_to_income`: enforce = **False** (fails 47.4% of approved applicants (at or above the 5% limit)).
- `max_loan_to_income`: enforce = **True** (fails 0.0% of approved applicants (below the 5% limit)).
- `max_loan_to_assets`: enforce = **True** (fails 0.0% of approved applicants (below the 5% limit)).
- `min_loan_term_years`: enforce = **True** (enforced by review decision).
- `max_loan_term_years`: enforce = **True** (enforced by review decision).

## Notes for a lender's credit-policy team to review

- `max_loan_to_income` (4.0) and `max_loan_to_assets` (1.5) reject nobody in this data: loan-to-income tops out at exactly 4.0 and loan-to-assets at about 1.33. They act as guards for inputs outside the training range, not as filters on it.
- The loan-term limits (2 to 20 years) match the data range exactly.
- `max_emi_to_income` is advisory only. A real FOIR-style cap would need the real interest rate and the applicant's existing EMIs, which the dataset lacks.
